Luggage Heritage & History

A Timeline of Travel Innovation

How 173 years of craftsmanship revolutionized the way humanity navigates the earth.

1853

Goyard (Paris)

Francois Goyard opens 233 Rue Saint-Honoré, pioneering hand-painted Goyardine chevron canvas trunks for European nobility and maharajas.

1854

Louis Vuitton (Paris)

Invention of the flat-topped grey Trianon waterproof canvas steamer trunk and the patented unpickable Tumbler Lock in Asnières-sur-Seine.

1897

Globe-Trotter (London)

Patent awarded for 14-layer vulcanized fibreboard luggage, earning the Royal Warrant and accompanying historic polar expeditions.

1898

Rimowa (Cologne)

Founded by Paul Morszeck. Later introduces the world’s first aviation-grade corrugated grooved aluminum suitcase inspired by the Junkers F13.

1910

Samsonite (Denver)

Jesse Shwayder introduces wooden trunks built so sturdy that five men could stand on them without crushing—ushering in modern durability.

1928

Aether Atelier (Zurich)

Swiss watchmaker tolerances applied to rivetless monocoque aerospace aluminum, forging the world’s most indestructible travel trunks.

1946

FPM Milano (Milan)

Fabbrica Pelletterie Milano introduces Italian luxury luggage, later teaming with Marc Sadler for the architectural Bank Collection.

1975

Tumi (New Jersey)

Introduces black-on-black military-grade FXT® Ballistic Nylon, redefining business travel luggage for the modern executive.

1993

Briggs & Riley (New York)

Pioneers the Outsider® handle and one-touch CX™ compression-expansion system, backed by an unconditional lifetime warranty.

2006

Montblanc #MY4810

Fuses German precision polycarbonate engineering with Florentine leather mastery for seamless international business navigation.

2011

Skybags (Mumbai)

Ignites a vibrant revolution in lightweight aerodynamic polycarbonate cases and commuter bags designed for high-velocity urban mobility.

2012

Carl Friedrik (London)

Scandinavian minimalism paired with vegetable-tanned Tuscan Vachetta leather from Santa Croce sull’Arno for timeless heirloom journeys.

2026

Aether 100-Year Warranty & Diagnostics

Launches the world’s first encrypted NFC lifetime warranty service with live 5-stage repair diagnostics across 180+ global service centers. Every handcrafted piece is registered to its owner and guaranteed for a century of journeys.
