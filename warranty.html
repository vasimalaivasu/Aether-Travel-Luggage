Frequently Answered Inquiries

What does the 100-Year Unconditional Warranty cover?
Our warranty covers all functional aspects of your luggage for 100 years, including cracked shells, broken locks, wheel failure, zipper malfunction, telescopic handle jamming, and transit damage caused by airlines. Cosmetic wear and scratches are not considered functional defects, though refinishing is available at our service centers.
How do I claim a repair when traveling abroad?
You can present your luggage at any of our worldwide service centers or contact our 24/7 Customer Support team. We will arrange a complimentary loaner piece while your luggage is serviced or ship replacement components directly to your hotel.
Are American Tourister, Skybags, Samsonite, and Rimowa pieces covered?
Yes! Every curated brand piece acquired through Aether Voyage receives our unified 100-Year Warranty Certificate in addition to the original manufacturer warranty.
